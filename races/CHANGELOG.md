# Changelog

A summary of significant changes to Apex by Milla, listed newest first.

---

## v2.1 — May 2026 (Stage 2 polish pass)

- Added a Sources section to every race report, grouped by category (Official Race Data, Tyre Strategy, Race Report & Driver Quotes, plus race-specific groups where relevant)
- Backfilled Sources sections retroactively for the Stage 1 race reports (Australia, China, Japan)
- Built a dedicated 1200×630 social-share image with solid dark background, so the logo is readable when the site URL is shared on Instagram, Twitter, or messaging platforms
- Added an independence/non-affiliation disclaimer to every page footer
- Cross-checked every engineering claim across all four race reports against the FIA 2026 Technical Regulations, F1's official explainer pages, and McLaren's regulations breakdown; corrected several specific values (tyre stint length, lap counts, terminology)
- Mobile-responsive fix: wrapped the Pace & Performance data table in a horizontally-scrollable container so it doesn't break layout on phones

---

## v2.0 — May 2026 (Stage 2 launch with Miami GP)

This is the inflection point where the analytical depth scales up.

- Added the Miami GP race report (Round 04) as the first Stage 2 post
- Introduced the Pace & Performance subsection with a data table comparing drivers by fastest lap, pit stop lap and time, and gap at flag
- Introduced the Engineering Question of the Race subsection, picking one specific pit-wall moment per race and dissecting the trade-off
- Introduced the Race Engineer's View subsection — my own pit-wall verdict on a key strategic call
- Added a Regulation Refinements subsection covering the FIA's April 2026 mid-season rule tweaks
- Added a date-based "NEW" announcement banner on the homepage that auto-fades to "LATEST" seven days after a race publishes
- Added a post-race penalty asterisk system: when a steward's decision changes the on-road order, affected drivers' cards now carry a small note explaining the change, with a footnote underneath
- Added a Miami signature accent colour to the race page hero (turquoise)
- Updated homepage to handle 4 completed races
